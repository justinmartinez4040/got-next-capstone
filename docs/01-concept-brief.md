# Concept Brief

## 1. Working name
Got Next

## 2. The pitch, in one sentence
A shared next-up queue for pickup basketball at the school gym — on a tablet at the court or an app on your phone — so people stop showing up blind and stop arguing over who called next.

## 3. Who it's for — specifically
Students who play pickup at the school gym, where there's no way to know who's already there or whose turn is next, and disputes over "I had next" happen constantly.

## 4. The job it does
When I get to the gym or I'm deciding whether to head over, I want to see who's playing and put myself in line for next, so I don't waste a trip and don't end up arguing about whose turn it is.

## 5. What people do instead today
Just show up and hope, then settle "who had next" by memory and arguing — no actual record of who called it.

## 6. Your biggest unknown
Whether people would actually use the tablet/app to check in for next, or just default back to yelling it out like they always have.
