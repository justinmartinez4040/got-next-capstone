# Concept Brief
git add docs/01-concept-brief.md
## 1. Working name
Got Next

## 2. The pitch, in one sentence
A courtside tool for pickup basketball at the school gym — a next-up queue plus a live crowd meter, so people can see how full it is before showing up, and stop arguing over who called next.

## 3. Who it's for — specifically
Students who play pickup at the school gym, where there's no way to know who's already there or whose turn is next, and disputes over "I had next" happen constantly.

## 4. The job it does
When I'm deciding whether to head to the gym, I want to check the crowd meter and see who's next in line, so I know right away if it's worth going or if I should just practice on my own — and once I'm there, I don't end up arguing about whose turn it is.

## 5. What people do instead today
Just show up and hope, then settle "who had next" by memory and arguing — no actual record of who called it.

## 6. Your biggest unknown
Whether people would actually use the tablet/app to check in for next, or just default back to yelling it out like they always have.
